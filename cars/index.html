How to read these pages

Each page uses EV Database's real-world efficiency rather than the official WLTP figure, adds 10% for charging losses, and prices the electricity at the tariff you choose. The default is Intelligent Octopus Go at 8p per kWh because it is the most widely used EV tariff; the standard price cap (26.32p) is shown alongside so you can see what switching is worth. Cost per mile is the cleanest way to compare cars with different battery sizes.

Estimates based on real-world efficiency data (EV Database) and tariff prices checked 29 September 2026. Tariff prices, eligibility and windows change – confirm with the supplier before switching. Some links may earn a commission at no cost to you; this never affects the figures.
