About charging the Kia EV3

Big battery in a small car: 78 kWh usable gives ~280 real miles.

Rapid charging is modest (128 kW) so home charging matters more than for rivals.

It has a heat pump as standard, so winter consumption typically rises 15–25% rather than the 30–40% you see on cars without one. The efficiency figure on this page is EV Database's real-world combined number, which sits between the official WLTP figure and a cold motorway run; it's the honest one to budget with.

On sale 2024–present. Home charging at 7 kW (11 kW AC on-board charger on this version) adds about 23 miles of range per hour. Public rapid charging peaks at 128 kW on the Long Range (81.4 kWh); at typical UK rapid-charger prices of 75–85p/kWh that works out at 24.5p per mile — roughly the same as petrol — so the whole saving with this car comes from charging at home on a tariff with a cheap window.

Which tariff suits a Kia EV3?

Charging 8,000 miles a year at home, the Kia EV3 needs about 7 kWh a night on average — 1h of charging at 7 kW — so it fits comfortably inside a five- or six-hour window. Our pick is EDF GoElectric: about £19.20 a month for charging, £422 a year less than the price cap. EDF GoElectric is fractionally cheaper per kWh and works with any charger, but has a higher peak rate and exit fees. If you can't charge overnight, OVO Charge Anytime gives 14p at any time of day.

Common questions

How much does it cost to fully charge a Kia EV3?

From empty, the Long Range (81.4 kWh) version needs about 87 kWh from the socket once charging losses are included. On Intelligent Octopus Go (8p) that is around £6.93; on the standard price cap it is around £23; at a 79p public rapid charger about £68.

What is the Kia EV3's cost per mile?

Using EV Database's real-world figure of 3.6 mi/kWh: about 2.5p per mile on an 8p EV tariff, 8.2p on the price cap and 24.5p on public rapid chargers. A 42 mpg petrol car at £1.38 a litre costs about 14.9p per mile.

How long does a Kia EV3 take to charge at home?

On a 7 kW home charger the 78 kWh battery goes from empty to full in roughly 12h 30m. Most nights you'll only be topping up 20–40%, which takes 3h 45m or so and fits inside any EV tariff window.

Which tariff is cheapest for a Kia EV3?

For an 8,000-mile-a-year driver charging at home, Utility Warehouse EV gives the lowest charging bill in our comparison, but it has conditions. EDF GoElectric is the best mainstream choice, costing about £19.20 a month for charging against £54 on the price cap.
